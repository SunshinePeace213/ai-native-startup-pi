# AI-native startup

> **In here:** An AI-native startup's defensible moat at Scale is built from accumulated depth · 5 claims, confidence 0.85.

## Current understanding

- A working prototype is not evidence of problem-solution fit — it is a pressure-testing prop for conversations with potential users, and those conversations are the real evidence (0.96)
- In an AI-native startup the founder stops being an individual contributor and becomes an orchestrator of agents, with attention shifting up the stack to generating ideas and directing the AI agents, tools, and small team that carry them out (0.84)
- An AI-native startup's defensible moat at Scale is built from accumulated depth — the expertise encoded into the product, the depth of integration with the other tools and platforms users rely on, and the proprietary system data and workflows — which is what makes consistent building in one direction genuinely hard to replicate (0.84)
- The AI-native startup journey remaps onto four core stages — Idea, MVP, Launch, and Scale — each carrying its own goal, exit criteria, and characteristic failure modes, replacing a traditional growth arc in which every new phase demanded a bigger team, a different skill set, and a fresh funding round (0.84)
- The false-passing-validator mistake, seen 10 times across shipped ledgers, is a check that passes without proving its criterion — extra rows, substring matches, or vacuous quantifiers let a wrong artifact through (0.76)
